Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

In short
  • Your notebooks stay on your iPad. Handwriting recognition and all math run on the device.
  • When you ask for a step-by-step explanation, the expression you asked about is sent as text to our server and to an AI provider, which writes the explanation. Your handwriting, pictures and other notebooks aren't sent.
  • There's no advertising and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Apple handles subscriptions and payments. We learn whether a subscription is active, not who you are.

This policy explains how The Famous Math Note (“the app”) handles information. “We” and “us” mean the developer of the app.

What stays on your iPad

Everything you create stays in the app's storage on your iPad:

WhatWhy
Notebooks: your handwriting, titles, pages, the readings and answers, and pictures and PDF pages you addSo your notebooks open as you left them
Samples of your handwriting, if you set up answers in your own handwriting (Essential, Premium and Lifetime Offline)To write answers in your handwriting. Recorded and used only on your iPad.
Corrections you make in “Did you mean”: the handwriting of that line and the reading you chose or typedSo the app remembers your correction in that notebook. These corrections also appear under Settings → Training data, where you can export them yourself.
Settings and tool choicesSo the app works the way you set it up

The app's documents folder is visible in the Files app, so you can see, copy and delete these files yourself. If your iPad is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, the backup may include this data. Apple handles backups under its own privacy policy, and we never receive them.

What leaves your iPad

When you ask for an explanation

Explanations are written by an AI model, which runs on servers rather than on your iPad. Nothing is sent until you ask for an explanation. When you do, the app sends over an encrypted connection:

  • the expression you asked about, as text (for example \int_0^1 (6^x - 5^x)\,dx), and the answer the app worked out;
  • any definitions from the same notebook that the expression uses (for example x = 3);
  • your app settings that change the math, such as degrees or radians.

The app doesn't send your handwriting, other expressions, pictures, PDFs or anything from other notebooks. The request goes to our server, which runs on Cloudflare, and the server passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the explanation with its Claude models. Anthropic processes requests for us to answer them and doesn't use them to train its models. It may keep them for a limited time under its own policies, for example to detect misuse (see Anthropic's privacy policy). We don't keep the content of your requests once the explanation has been delivered.

Please don't include personal information in an expression you ask to have explained.

Plan and usage information

Each plan includes a number of explanations. To check your plan and count what you've used, the app sends our server, with each explanation request:

  • a random identifier the app creates and keeps on your iPad;
  • Apple's signed record that the app came from the App Store, which includes an identifier Apple assigns to your download of the app, its version and dates;
  • if you've bought a plan or AI Pass, Apple's signed record of each active purchase: which product it is, its transaction identifiers and its purchase and expiry dates.

Apple signs these records, so our server can confirm your plan without asking the app to vouch for it. They don't include your name, email address, Apple ID or payment details.

Our server keeps a counter of how many explanations you've used, stored under your purchase's transaction identifier (on a paid plan or AI Pass), Apple's download identifier, or the app's random identifier (on Free). Monthly counters are deleted after the following month, and any counter is deleted after 12 months without an explanation request.

Like any server, ours sees the IP address a request comes from. On Free, we count requests per IP address per day to stop automated abuse; those counts are deleted within two days. We don't use IP addresses to identify you or to track you.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've turned on “Share With App Developers” in your iPad's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects these, and they're governed by Apple's privacy policy. They don't include your notebooks.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple handles downloads, subscriptions, payments and refunds through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. We only learn, through Apple's signed records described above, which plan or AI Pass is active and when it renews or ends.

Photos and files

When you add a picture, the app uses the system photo picker. Only the picture you choose is given to the app, and the app doesn't ask for access to your photo library. Pictures you paste, and PDFs you import, are copied into the notebook on your device and are never sent anywhere by the app.

Exporting and sharing

When you export a notebook as a PDF or export training data, the app opens the system share sheet. You choose where the file goes (for example Files, Mail or AirDrop). From then on, that service's or recipient's privacy practices apply.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share data with data brokers.
  • We don't use your requests to train AI models.

Children

The app is meant for students, including younger ones. We don't ask for names, ages, email addresses or any other personal information, and explanation requests contain only math. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age set by local law). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • To delete a notebook, delete it in the library. It stays in the “Deleted Notebooks” folder for 30 days, then it's erased. To erase it sooner, delete it from that folder in the Files app.
  • To remove everything the app has stored on your iPad, delete the app. This also removes the random identifier. Its usage record on our server is no longer linked to anything and is deleted after 12 months.
  • If you'd rather nothing leave your iPad, don't use explanations. Everything else works offline.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA may give you the right to access or delete information about you. Because we don't know who you are, contact us with the identifier shown in the app's Settings → About, and we'll delete its usage record.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If a change means more data would leave your iPad, the app will tell you first.
