Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get an answer?

Write an expression and finish it with “=”. Then lift the pen. After a short pause the answer appears next to the “=”. You can set the pause in Settings → Handwriting. Equations such as 2x + 5 = 11 are solved without a trailing “=”. You can turn that off in Settings → Math.

The first launch says “Preparing handwriting model…”

After you install or update the app, iPadOS prepares the handwriting model for your iPad once. This can take a few minutes. You can already write in the meantime, and answers appear once the model is ready. Later launches are quick.

It read my handwriting wrong. What can I do?

Tap the answer, or tap or long-press the expression with your finger, to open “Did you mean”. Choose one of the other readings, or edit the LaTeX directly. Your correction is remembered in that notebook. An orange dot marks a reading the app was unsure of. Writing a little larger, and leaving clear space between separate expressions, also helps.

How do variables work across the page?

Write a definition such as x = 3, f(x) = x² + 1, or a matrix A = […]. Then use it anywhere in the same notebook. If a name is defined twice, the nearest definition wins.

What math does it understand?

Arithmetic, equations and systems, factoring, trigonometry, units, number bases, functions, limits, derivatives, integrals, series, vector calculus, matrices and statistics. The Handbook (the book icon in a notebook) lists every function, how to write it, and examples.

Where are my notebooks saved? How do I back them up?

On your iPad, inside the app. You can see them in the Files app under “On My iPad”. iCloud or computer backups of your iPad include them. You can also export any notebook as a PDF with the share button.

Can I write with my finger?

Yes. Turn on Settings → Appearance → Draw with finger, or show the finger-drawing button in the notebook. When finger drawing is off, a finger tap selects pictures and opens “Did you mean”.

If I delete the app, are my notebooks gone?

Yes. Notebooks are stored only inside the app on your iPad, so deleting the app deletes them, unless you have an iCloud or computer backup of your iPad. Before deleting the app, export the notebooks you want to keep as PDFs, or copy them out of the Files app. Updating the app from the App Store keeps your notebooks.

I deleted a notebook by mistake.

A deleted notebook moves to the “Deleted Notebooks” folder and stays there for 30 days. After that it's erased. To get it back within those 30 days, open the Files app and go to On My iPad → Math Note → Deleted Notebooks. Then move the notebook's folder back into the Notebooks folder.

Does the app need the internet, or collect my data?

Writing, recognition and answers all work offline on your iPad. Only AI explanations need the internet. When you ask for one, the expression you asked about is sent as text to be explained, not your handwriting or notebooks. See the Privacy Policy.

Plans and explanations

What do the plans include?

Free includes 3 notebooks, PDF import and export (exported PDFs carry a small “Made with The Famous Math Note”), and 5 AI explanations to try. Essential ($19.99 a year, about $1.67 a month, or $2.99 a month) adds unlimited notebooks, answers written in your own handwriting, PDF exports without a watermark, and 30 explanations a month. Premium ($39.99 a year, about $3.33 a month, or $6.99 a month) adds 150 explanations a month from frontier reasoning models. Yearly plans start with a 7-day free trial. Lifetime Offline ($29.99 once) gives unlimited notebooks, your own handwriting and watermark-free PDFs for good, with 10 starter explanations. The app shows how many explanations you have left. See Pricing.

How do I ask for an explanation?

Tap an answer and choose Explain. The steps appear next to it. You need an internet connection. On Essential and Premium your allowance resets at the start of each month, and unused explanations don't carry over. The explanations included with Free and Lifetime Offline are a one-time allowance.

How do I subscribe, change plans or cancel?

To subscribe, open Settings → Plan in the app. To change plans or cancel, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPad. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

I subscribed, but the app still shows Free.

Open Settings → Plan in the app and tap Restore Purchases. This also brings your plan back after you reinstall the app or set up a new iPad with the same Apple ID.

What happens to my notebooks if my subscription ends?

You keep all of them, and you can open, edit and export every one. If you have more than 3, you can't create a new notebook until you have fewer than 3. Answers go back to the standard font.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
