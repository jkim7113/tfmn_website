5. Answers and explanations can be wrong

The app turns handwriting into math with a machine-learning model and then computes a result automatically. Either step can go wrong. A symbol can be misread, a value can be rounded, or an expression can be understood differently from what you meant. AI explanations can also contain mistakes, including steps that look convincing but are wrong.

The app is a study and note-taking aid. Don't rely on it alone for anything where a mistake could cause harm or loss, such as engineering, medical, financial, legal or safety decisions, or graded work you haven't checked. Always verify important results yourself.
