What it does

A notebook first. A calculator when you want one.

Write the way you would on paper. Answers appear only where you finish an expression with “=”.

Reads your handwriting

A handwriting model that reads your pen strokes, built for math: fractions, roots, powers, integrals, matrices and systems. An orange dot marks a reading it's unsure of.

Answers beside the “=”

Answers appear in your answer color at handwriting size, and many expressions can sit anywhere on the page. With Essential or Premium, answers are written in your own handwriting.

The page remembers

Define x = 3, a function or a matrix once and use it anywhere below. If something is defined twice, the nearest definition wins.

Step-by-step explanations

Ask how an answer was found, and AI walks you through each step. Explanations need an internet connection and are included in every plan, with more each month on Essential and Premium.

Did you mean

Tap an answer to check the reading. You can pick another reading or edit the LaTeX with a live preview. Your corrections are remembered for each notebook.

Pages or an endless canvas

Choose US-Letter pages or an infinite canvas, with blank, lined, grid or dot paper in white, cream or dark. Import PDFs and write on them, add pictures, and export as PDF.

Private by design

Handwriting recognition and math run on your iPad. Nothing you write leaves it unless you ask for an explanation.

Tools that stay out of the way

Pen, a note pen the math reader skips, highlighter, erasers, lasso and ruler. Each tool keeps three saved sizes. Hold the pen still at the end of a stroke to snap it into a shape.

Statistics, drawn

Write a list of numbers to get summaries, t and χ² tests, regression and distributions. Results come back as tidy tables, and histograms and box plots are drawn on the page.

Privacy

Your notebooks stay on your iPad.

Reading your handwriting and working out answers happen on the device. There's no advertising, no tracking, and we never sell data.

On-device recognition

The handwriting model and the math engine are built into the app and run offline.

AI only when you ask

An explanation sends only the expression you asked about, as text, to be explained. Your handwriting, pictures and other notebooks stay on your iPad.

Your files, your choice

Notebooks are saved on the iPad and appear in the Files app. Nothing is shared unless you export it yourself.
